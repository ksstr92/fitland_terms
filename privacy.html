This policy explains how Fitland handles information when you use the Fitland mobile application and related services (the “Service”). We aim to use only the information needed to provide the experience and to give you meaningful control over it.

Terms of Use

2. Information we may handle

  • Account information: If account sign-in is enabled, an account identifier and authentication/session information provided through Apple, Google, or the configured account service. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Island and game progress: Island name, a seed and generation version used to recreate your island, saved choices, progress totals, and settings needed to sync your game.
  • Activity summaries: If you choose to connect Apple Health/HealthKit or Android Health Connect, Fitland may read step totals and, only if separately supported and permissioned, eligible workout-minute totals. The app is designed to use daily aggregates; it does not need GPS routes, heart rate, calories, or raw health samples for island growth.
  • Social sharing: If you choose to share an island, invitation and access records and the island snapshot made available to invited visitors. A snapshot may show your island name, appearance choices, and cumulative growth; it is designed not to include exact step totals, workout details, goals, device information, or location.
  • Support and diagnostics: Information you choose to send when contacting support, plus limited technical information required to operate and protect the Service. Any analytics or crash-reporting tools, and the data they collect, should be disclosed here before they are enabled.

3. Health and fitness information

Health access is optional and requires your permission through the operating system. You can decline or revoke permission in your device settings. Without access, activity-based features may be unavailable; your island remains yours. Fitland uses permitted activity summaries to calculate in-game progress, not to provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or assess your health.

We do not sell health information or use it for advertising. Health information is not included in social island snapshots. When syncing is enabled, the minimum activity summary needed for rewards may be sent securely to the service backend and associated with your account. Platform permissions and actual behavior are controlled by the shipped app version.

4. How we use information

We use information to provide and sync your island, calculate progress, remember your choices, enable sharing you request, keep the Service secure, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. We do not use health information to target advertisements.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We may process information through providers that host the backend, authenticate accounts, or deliver the Service. If island sharing is enabled, people you invite can see the specific island snapshot described above. Sharing is optional; invitations can be revoked and access can be blocked where those controls are available. A shared island’s growth may suggest activity over time even though visitors cannot see your exact activity records.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users or the Service, or respond to a valid legal request. If the operator or Service is reorganized or transferred, information may transfer as part of that transaction subject to applicable privacy requirements.

6. Storage, security, and retention

We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information in transit and in the systems we operate. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Account-linked records are retained while needed to provide the Service and then deleted or de-identified according to operational and legal requirements. Backups may retain deleted records for a limited period before they are overwritten. The operator should publish concrete retention periods before launch.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Choose whether to grant health permissions and revoke them through your device settings.
  • Keep your island private, and revoke invitations or block access where sharing controls are available.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of account data by contacting the privacy contact above.
  • Delete your account in the app when that feature is available. The planned deletion flow removes account-linked profile, island, activity, invitation, access, and related service records, subject to the backup retention described above.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact the operator to make a request; we may need to verify your identity before acting.

8. Children

Fitland is not intended for children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information, contact the operator so it can be reviewed and removed as appropriate. Before launch, the operator must set the intended age range and comply with applicable child privacy requirements.

9. International processing and changes

Your information may be processed in countries where the operator or its service providers operate. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used. We may update this policy as the Service changes. We will change the effective date above and provide additional notice for material changes when required.
